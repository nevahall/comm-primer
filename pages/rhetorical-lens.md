#  The Fourth Intention: Intentio Algorithmi

When people communicate, there are several things that influence what a message means. First, there is what the sender wants to say. Second, there is the actual message and the words being used. Third, there is how the person receiving the message understands it. Umberto Eco explored these three ideas when explaining how people create and interpret meaning. They are known as the author's intention, the meaning of the message itself, and the reader's interpretation.

With AI becoming more involved in communication, there is another influence to consider. This is called intentio algorithmi, or the fourth intention. It describes how AI can influence the meaning of a message by choosing the words and tone used to communicate it.

For example, imagine an employee asks AI to write an email explaining that they cannot finish a project today but will have it completed tomorrow. The employee might only want to give a quick update. However, AI could create a long email that includes several apologies, formal language, and a detailed explanation. Although the main information is still correct, the email might make the situation seem more serious than it actually is.

This is what makes the fourth intention important. The employee begins with an idea, AI decides how to express that idea, and the receiver interprets the final message. AI becomes another influence on communication because it can change the tone or meaning of the original idea. 

Understanding intentio algorithmi helps explain why people should not automatically assume an AI-written email perfectly represents their thoughts. This is especially important when writing about topics like apologies, disagreements, giving feedback, and especially workplace emails. AI can help organize a message and make it sound professional, but the person sending it should still make sure it communicates what they mean.
