# The Phenomenon: Writing Workplace Emails 

Artificial intelligence (AI) is becoming a common part of how people communicate in their personal and professional lives. One way people use AI is to help write emails. Instead of writing an entire email themselves, someone can give AI a short instruction and have it create a complete message. AI can also rewrite emails to make them sound more professional, friendly, or persuasive. In everyday life, someone might use AI to write an email to a professor or respond to a difficult message. In the workplace, employees might use AI to communicate with coworkers, supervisors, or customers. Although AI makes writing easier, it also changes how people create and send messages.

One of the biggest benefits of using AI to write emails is that it saves time. Employees often have several responsibilities throughout the day, and writing emails can take up a lot of time. AI can help employees respond to messages more quickly, allowing them to focus on other tasks. It can also help people who struggle with writing or are unsure of how to say something professionally. For example, an employee who needs to explain a mistake to their supervisor might use AI to organize their thoughts and create a respectful email. This can make difficult conversations feel easier to handle.

However, AI-written emails can also create problems. Sometimes, AI produces messages that sound professional but do not reflect how the sender would normally communicate. For example, an employee might ask AI to write a short email explaining that a meeting has been canceled. Instead of writing something simple, AI might create a long email with overly formal language. Even though the information is correct, the message might sound unnatural to the person receiving it.

This is important because communication is about more than just sharing information. The way people communicate affects their relationships and how others view them. In the workplace, an email can influence whether someone seems trustworthy, respectful, or professional. In personal life, an AI-written message might make a conversation feel less genuine. As AI becomes more common, it is important to understand whether it is simply helping people communicate or actually changing what their messages mean.
