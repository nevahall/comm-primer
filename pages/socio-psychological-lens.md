# What the Reader Should Do

One of the most important things to remember is that AI should be used as a writing assistant rather than a replacement for human communication. AI can help organize thoughts, correct grammar, and make emails easier to understand. However, people should still think about what they want to say before asking AI to write something. The Shannon-Weaver model reminds us that information needs to be communicated clearly, while semiotics and encoding/decoding show that people can interpret the same words in different ways.

In the workplace, employees should always read through AI-written emails before sending them. They should check for incorrect information, unnecessary details, and language that does not sound like something they would normally say. They should also think about who will receive the email. For example, an email to a supervisor might need to sound more formal than an email to a close coworker. Employees should make changes to AI-generated messages so they fit their workplace and relationships. They should also be careful about sharing private company or customer information with AI tools.

In personal life, people should be especially careful when using AI to write emotional or important messages. AI might help someone figure out how to start a difficult conversation, but it does not fully understand the relationship between the people involved.

Overall, AI can be a useful tool for improving communication, but people need to stay involved in the writing process of whatever they are creating. To have good communication, you need to make the the receiver understands the message the sender is trying to convey. The ideas discussed in this primer show that communication involves sharing information, creating meaning, and understanding different interpretations. As AI becomes more common in personal and professional life, people should use it to support their own communication rather than allowing it to completely replace their voice.
